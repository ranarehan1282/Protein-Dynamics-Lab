Put student photos here, for example: hifza.jpg, najaf.jpg
Then in team.js set photo:"images/hifza.jpg" for that person.

Recommended: a square photo (1:1), at least 400x400px. The site crops to a
square box automatically, so a portrait or landscape photo also works, but a
roughly centred face gives the best crop.

Always get the student's consent before publishing their photo.
