Join us

We welcome MS and PhD students, and collaborators, in computational biology and bioinformatics.

Who we are looking for

Curious students with a background in bioinformatics, biochemistry, biotechnology, chemistry, physics or computer science. Interest in molecular simulation is a plus. Programming experience helps but can be learned.

MS and PhD students

Admission runs through the university. Check the NCB admissions page for programmes, dates and eligibility, then contact us about fit.

How to apply to the lab

  1. Email Dr. Khalid with the subject "Prospective student".
  2. Attach your CV and transcript.
  3. Write one paragraph on which project interests you and why.
  4. Optional: a report, code sample or thesis abstract.
Email Dr. Khalid

Current openings

HEC International Research Support Initiative Programme (IRSIP), Batch 60 is now open to eligible PhD students. Deadline: 30 October 2026. See eligibility criteria and apply here.

Collaborators and industry

We are open to joint projects. Email us with a short outline. Areas where we can help:

Protein structure modelling

Homology models, cryo-EM map fitting and structural analysis.

Molecular dynamics

Simulations of conformational change, allostery and ligand binding.

Virtual screening

Docking, pharmacophore screening and rescoring for hit finding.

Omics data analysis

Computational analysis of proteomic and biological datasets.
